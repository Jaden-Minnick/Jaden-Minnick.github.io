At a glance

Curiosity with a purpose.

I am a Nuclear Engineering Ph.D. student at the University of Tennessee, Knoxville. My current research focuses on uranium chloride molten salts for advanced fission reactors, within a broader interest in molten salt chemical engineering for both fission and fusion.

My long-term goal is to advance efficient, economically practical nuclear energy systems and contribute to closing the nuclear fuel cycle, through research, collaboration, and public understanding.
